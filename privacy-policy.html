Privacy Policy

Parcel-PT — Shopify shipping app for CTT logistics
Effective date: 26 August 2026
Last updated: 26 August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how CondoLink ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you install or use Parcel-PT (the "App"), a Shopify application that helps merchants create CTT shipping labels, calculate checkout rates, and manage fulfillments.

By installing or using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the App.

1. Who this policy applies to

  • Merchants — Shopify store owners or staff who install and use the App in Shopify Admin.
  • End customers — people who buy from a merchant's store. Parcel-PT processes limited customer data on behalf of the merchant to provide shipping services.

For end-customer personal data, the merchant is typically the data controller and CondoLink acts as a data processor, except where we determine purposes and means of processing (for example, billing and platform security).

2. Information we collect

2.1 Merchant and shop information

  • Shop domain, shop name, and installation timestamps
  • Shopify OAuth session data, including access tokens required to operate the App
  • Shopify staff profile data available through OAuth (such as name, email, locale, and account role)
  • App settings you configure (shipping origin address, phone numbers, default shipping methods, billing plan, and carrier service preferences)

2.2 Order and shipping information

To create shipments, labels, and tracking updates, we process order-related data from Shopify, including:

  • Shopify order IDs, order names, and fulfillment identifiers
  • Shipping addresses (recipient name, street, city, postal code, country, phone)
  • Customer email addresses associated with orders, when provided by Shopify
  • Package weight, carrier service, tracking numbers, and shipment status
  • Delivery instructions submitted at checkout through the App's checkout extension (stored as cart/order metafields)
  • Shipping rate request data at checkout (destination country/postal code and cart weight)

2.3 Billing and wallet information

  • Selected subscription or usage-based billing plan
  • Label usage records and postage credit top-ups processed through Shopify Billing
  • Internal wallet ledger entries (amounts, references, and timestamps)

Payment card details are handled by Shopify; we do not receive or store full payment card numbers.

2.4 Technical and operational data

  • Webhook payloads from Shopify and our logistics integration partners
  • Application logs, error messages, and idempotency records
  • IP addresses and request metadata in server logs (for security and troubleshooting)

3. How we use information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the App
  • Authenticate your shop and communicate with Shopify on your behalf
  • Create shipping labels, transmit shipment data to carriers, and update fulfillments and tracking in Shopify
  • Calculate and return CTT shipping rates at checkout
  • Process billing, usage charges, and postage credits
  • Respond to merchant support requests and comply with legal obligations
  • Improve reliability, security, and performance of the App

We do not sell personal information.

4. Legal bases (EEA/UK merchants)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on:

  • Contract — to provide the App and shipping integration you request
  • Legitimate interests — to secure the service, prevent abuse, and improve operations
  • Legal obligation — where required by applicable law
  • Consent — where required for optional features or communications

5. How we share information

Recipient Purpose
Shopify App platform, OAuth, Admin API, webhooks, billing, and checkout/carrier service integrations
CondoLink logistics services Backend shipment creation, tracking, and CTT API orchestration on our infrastructure
CTT (Correios de Portugal) Shipping label creation, parcel handling, and delivery services
Google Cloud Hosting (Cloud Run), database (Cloud SQL), and related infrastructure in the EU where configured
Professional advisers and authorities When required for legal compliance, dispute resolution, or protection of rights

We share only the data reasonably necessary for these purposes. Sub-processors are bound by contractual data protection obligations where applicable.

6. International transfers

Data may be processed in the European Economic Area and, where service providers operate elsewhere, we implement appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) as required by applicable law.

7. Data retention

  • Active shops: we retain data while the App is installed and as needed to provide the service.
  • After uninstall: Shopify sends a shop/redact compliance webhook; we delete shop records, sessions, and associated app data within the timelines required by Shopify's Partner Program.
  • Customer redaction: when Shopify sends customers/redact, we remove or anonymize linked shipment identifiers and customer-related fields we store.
  • Logs and backups: may be retained for a limited period for security and disaster recovery, then deleted or anonymized.

8. Your rights and Shopify compliance webhooks

The App implements Shopify mandatory compliance webhooks: customers/data_request, customers/redact, and shop/redact.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or port personal data, and to object to certain processing. End customers should contact the merchant first; merchants may contact us to assist with requests relating to data processed through the App.

9. Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational measures appropriate to the risk, including encrypted transport (HTTPS), access controls, secret management, and least-privilege service credentials. CTT carrier credentials are not exposed to browsers or merchant clients.

10. Children

The App is a business tool for merchants and is not directed at children under 16.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes may also be communicated through the App or Shopify App Store listing where appropriate.
